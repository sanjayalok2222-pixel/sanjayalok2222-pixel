## 🏆 Achievements & Recognition

### 🥇 Robotics Competition Wins
I have participated in multiple robotics events and competitions and have won robotics events. Through these challenges, I have gained extensive hands-on experience in:
- **Robot Design & Kinematics**: Building robust structural designs optimized for speed and stability.
- **Motor Control & Actuation**: Working with PWM control, H-bridges, and motor driver configuration.
- **Sensor Integration**: Deploying IR, ultrasonic, and other sensors for environmental feedback loops.
- **Wireless / RC Systems**: Designing wireless control telemetry for remote operations.
- **Real-Time Troubleshooting**: Diagnosing and resolving system faults under high-pressure competition environments.
- **Team-Based Competition**: Collaborating with multi-disciplinary engineering teams to design, build, and test systems.
